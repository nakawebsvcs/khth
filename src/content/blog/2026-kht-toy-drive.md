The season of giving is here, and we need your help to make the holidays brighter for keiki across Hawaiʻi!

**Last year, thanks to the incredible generosity of our community, Kids Hurt Too Hawaiʻi provided Christmas gifts to more than 480 children!** This year, we're hoping to reach even MORE!

From **November 1 through December 13**, we're collecting **NEW, UNOPENED TOYS of all kinds and for all ages** to bring joy, hope, and a little holiday magic to keiki and families in our community.

Every toy is more than just a gift. It's a reminder to a child that they are loved, supported, and never alone.

**TOY DRIVE DETAILS**

* **Dates:** November 1 – December 13, 2026
* **Donations:** New, unopened toys for all ages
* **Goal:** Bring holiday joy to even more than 480 keiki!

Whether you're an individual, family, business, or community organization, your generosity can make a difference!

**Let's come together in the spirit of aloha and spread joy, one toy at a time!**

For more information about donating, contact **[info@kidshurttoo.org](mailto:info@kidshurttoo.org)**.
