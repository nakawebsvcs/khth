We’re excited to announce that **Surf for the Soul is becoming a monthly event!** Join us every **third Sunday of the month at Ala Moana Beach Park** for a morning of ocean fun, connection, and learning.

Hosted in partnership with **Ikaika Hawaiʻi Waterman Academy**, Surf for the Soul gives keiki the opportunity to build confidence in and around the ocean while learning important **ocean safety and water skills** in a supportive environment.

Together, we’re helping keiki **ride the waves of life**—building resilience, making connections, and having fun alongside a caring community.

**When:** Every 3rd Sunday of the month
**Where:** Ala Moana Beach Park
**Hosted by:** Ikaika Hawaiʻi Waterman Academy & Kids Hurt Too Hawaiʻi
